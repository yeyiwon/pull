# PULL Alive & Cute — Resident 01

## Mallow, the seed keeper

Mallow is a small non-human garden resident, not another Moru. The silhouette is a low pear-shaped body, two long leaf-like ears, short quick legs, a seed satchel and a softly luminous tail bud. The face uses only two dark eyes and an expressive ear line so it remains readable on an iPhone.

Mallow moves in short purposeful trips between the porch, Home garden and river bank. In clear daytime it inspects plants and carries seeds. In rain it hurries beneath the porch. At night it pauses near the river and looks upward. It notices Moru and the player with a small wave, but does not wait in place as a dialogue kiosk.

The v1 action vocabulary is `IDLE`, `WALK`, `RUN`, `LOOK`, `INSPECT`, `REACT`, `WAVE`, `CARRY` and `PLACE`. The Lost Seeds slice uses walking, looking, reacting and carrying. Future event gathering can reuse the same world-space routine without adding an LLM.
