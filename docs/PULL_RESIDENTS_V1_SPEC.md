# PULL Residents v1

## Mallow — Garden keeper

- Silhouette: round cream body, leaf ears, side seed bag
- Expression: soft eyes, visible blush, small delayed reactions
- Movement: measured walk, tiny bounce, pauses beside flowers
- Temperament: kind and slightly forgetful
- Favorite place: Home Garden
- Life: waters plants, watches butterflies, carries seeds, rests by the porch

## Pebble — River wanderer

- Silhouette: low blue-grey body, round ears, warm scarf and shell pouch
- Expression: wide curious eyes and quick head turns
- Movement: leisurely wandering interrupted by short excited runs
- Temperament: curious and easily fascinated
- Favorite place: Old Bridge and river edge
- Life: watches water, inspects stones, collects one small shell at a time

## Lumi — Little maker

- Silhouette: lavender body, longer ears, warm square lantern
- Expression: calm eyes, careful pauses, small surprised lean
- Movement: slow deliberate steps while carrying objects
- Temperament: quiet and meticulous, with occasional harmless mistakes
- Favorite place: Home Porch
- Life: carries and places lanterns, checks objects, sits after finishing

## Situations

1. Ambient — Lumi briefly drops a carried lantern part, reacts, picks it up, and continues. No player action is required.
2. Interactive — Pebble stops at the river to watch a small glint. Approaching and talking shares the observation.
3. Request — Mallow's existing Lost Seeds request remains the single short request and preserves its current reward and persistence.

All decisions are local and deterministic. A shared low-frequency routine chooses activities from time, weather, previous activity, favorite place and controlled variation. Rendering and locomotion remain in one frame loop for all three residents.
